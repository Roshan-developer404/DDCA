COMBINATIONAL TRUTH-TABLE & HALF/FULL ADDER REFERENCE

1-Bit Half Adder vs. Full Adder Equations

Half Adder (HA): Sum = A ⊕ B Carry = A · B Full Adder (FA): Sum = A ⊕ B ⊕ Cin Cout = (A · B) + (Cin · (A ⊕ B)) Two's-Complement Subtraction (A - B): Invert B (B' = B ⊕ Sub) and set Cin = Sub = 1 → A + ~B + 1
SEQUENTIAL TIMING & CHARACTERISTIC EQUATIONS

Latches, Flip-Flops & Timing Constraints

SR Latch: Q(next) = S + (~R · Q) [Constraint: S · R = 0] D Latch: Q(next) = D [Transparent while EN = 1] D Flip-Flop: Q(t+1) = D(t) [Sampled strictly on CLK ↑] Timing Margin Equation: T_clk ≥ t_pcq (Clock-to-Q) + t_pd (Max Combinational Delay) + t_su (Setup Time)
