# Source Material & Asset Attribution

## 1. Primary Educational Course Material
The curriculum progression, module topics, worked instruction traces (Programs A, B, and C), practical laboratory experiments, and the 12 exported lecture slide diagrams in `assets/source-slides/` derive from the user-supplied **Digital Design & Computer Architecture (DDCA)** course materials (`S01` through `S88` lecture decks and practical design manuals):

- `S01_Introduction_4_-02.png` — Module 1 (Introduction to Digital Design & Computer Architecture)
- `S07_Logic_gates2_page6.png` — Module 1 (Logic gates in a single-cycle RISC-V processor)
- `S18_to_S20_Combinational_and_K-Maps2_page8.png` — Module 2 (Karnaugh-map simplification)
- `S33_and_S34__Sequential_Logic_Circuits2_page5.png` — Module 3 (Sequential state and clocked behavior)
- `S49_and_S50_Register_File2_page4.png` & `page6.png` — Module 4 (Register-file organization and implementation)
- `S51_to_S58_RAM_ROM2_page10.png` — Module 4 (Memory connected to the datapath)
- `S72_to_S74_Single_cycle_data_path_and_control_unit1_page6.png` — Module 5 (Single-cycle datapath and control unit)
- `S75-S79__Complete,_Run_and_Extend_the_Single-Cycle_RISC-V_Processor1_page4.png` & `page8.png` — Module 6 (Program 1 & Program 2 execution traces)
- `S80-S82_Memory_Mapped_IO1_page5.png` — Module 6 (Memory-mapped peripheral / LED example)
- `S83-S88_From_Working_CPU_to_Faster_CPU1_page11.png` — Module 6 (Processor performance improvements)
